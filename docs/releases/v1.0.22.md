# Version 1.0 - Technical Build 1.0.22

Owner approved the local group-contributions QA candidate for production on 15 October 2026, before commits or deployment.

## Changes

- FR-PAY-032: payments from any member of an active saved payment group contribute toward that group's dues. The configured default payer no longer restricts ordinary Credit coverage.
- Apply the policy in the canonical ledger, shared by Sessions, Player Balances, group cards, Dashboard and summaries. Existing receipts remain unchanged; their available funding is recalculated without creating cash or transferring ownership.
- Keep unused Credit/Advance owned by its contributor, preserve source chronology, and spend each source at most once across overlapping groups. Unrelated players and inactive groups remain isolated.
- Group history includes its members' ungrouped individual and Advance receipts, including reversals. Original receipt details remain auditable. Cards say Default payer rather than implying that person made every payment.
- Individual payment confirmation acknowledges the recorded amount without implying that Credit used immediately for group dues remains unspent.
