# Version 1.0 - Technical Build 1.0.18

Owner approved the local QA candidate for production on 27 September 2026.

## Changes

- Player and payment-group Due Reminders follow the Advance statement layout: Contributions, Total Contributions, Usage by member, member totals, Total Used, and Amount Due or Credit Remaining. Remaining Advance is retained when applicable.
- The reminder covers the current unsettled period. A cleared earlier period drops out when later usage starts; earlier unpaid charges remain in the same period. A later partial payment must not incorrectly hide older usage.
- Prior excess payments appear as Credit carried forward. Payments, Credit, Advance and direct activity payments are attributed to their sources without changing ownership or allocation rules. Personal reminders do not claim another member's unused balance.
- Reversed receipts do not contribute; reversing a clearing payment reopens the earlier statement period. Same-day charges and funding are evaluated together at the date granularity of the stored records. Legacy undated settlements retain a neutral recorded-settlement label, not an invented payer or payment date.
- Activity usage includes the payer's directly covered own share once, with matching contribution evidence. Summary generation does not write records, create receipts, change balances or transfer funds.
- Positive-due reminders end with: "Please share the payment via Aani. If not available, DM me for A/C details." This request is omitted when no payment is due. The generated-via-app footer remains.
- The separate full Summary, Advance statements, payment history and allocation engine retain their existing behavior.

## Verification

- 226 automated tests passed on the owner-approved candidate, including six new period-scope/reconciliation tests and the earlier reminder-contribution/Aani coverage.
- Tests cover cleared weeks, retained earlier dues, partial payments after multiple sessions, carried-forward excess with either due or Credit left, reversals, private member Credit, group/individual scope, direct activity usage and repeated payments/Advances.
- State immutability and reconciliation are asserted: usage minus contributions equals due minus remaining Credit and Advance. The underlying ledger remains authoritative.
- Local-only QA used synthetic sample records with outbound app connections blocked. No production dataset was imported or mutated for this patch.
- Mobile previews checked at 390 and 320 pixels, with no horizontal overflow; contribution lines, usage, totals and copy controls remain accessible. No browser warning/error was observed.
